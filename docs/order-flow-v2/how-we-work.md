# How WashBee works (customer-facing copy)

Used for the "How we work" screen on Home. Images are produced separately; English only. For 6 steps, merge steps 4 and 5.

| # | Title | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Book in a minute | Pick your services, choose per kg or per item, and set your pickup time. Choose Express if you need it now. |
| 2 | We come to your door | A nearby pickup partner is assigned. Track them live on the map. |
| 3 | Counted in front of you | Your clothes are counted or weighed at your door. Your bill appears in the app, and you don't pay yet. |
| 4 | Checked at our hub | Our team verifies your order and sends each item to the right specialist. |
| 5 | Cleaned with care | Each service, such as wash, iron or dry clean, is done by a dedicated expert. |
| 6 | Ready and on its way | When everything is done, a delivery partner brings it back to you. You see their name and live location. |
| 7 | Pay on delivery | Pay by UPI in the app, or hand cash to the delivery partner. Your clothes are handed over once you've paid. |
